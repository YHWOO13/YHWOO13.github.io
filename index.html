About

Building grounded AI systems
for human understanding

I am a Ph.D. student in Computer and Information Technology at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Julia T. Rayz. My research sits at the intersection of Knowledge Graphs, Large Language Models, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

I am interested in how structured knowledge can help AI systems reason more reliably, maintain semantic coherence, and adapt to users with different knowledge backgrounds. Outside research, I am a Manchester United supporter and Korea national team fan.
