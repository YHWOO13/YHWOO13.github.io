I'm a Ph.D. student in Computer and Information Technology at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Julia T. Rayz. My research sits at the intersection of Knowledge Graphs, Large Language Models, and Retrieval-Augmented Generation.

I aim to build human-centered NLP applications — intelligent systems that reason over structured knowledge with deep semantic understanding, making AI genuinely useful for people.
